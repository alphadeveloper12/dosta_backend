# Dosta Ramadan Menus 2026

## Overview
Dosta provides premium catering services for Ramadan, including Iftar and Sohour menus. These are suitable for corporate and private events.

## Iftar Menus

### Iftar Menu A
- **Price:** 185 AED per person
- **Inclusions:**
  - **Ramadan Beverages:** Jallab, Kamardine, Laban, Rose Water.
  - **Cold Mezze & Salads:** Hummus, Moutabal, Mouhamara, Fattoush, Tabbouleh, Baba Ghanouj, Beetroot Mutabal, Labneh with Garlic, Warak Enab, Shanklish, Eggplant Moutabal.
  - **Fresh Salad Bar:** Greek Salad, Quinoa Salad, Mexican Salad.
  - **Hot Mezze:** Kibbeh, Cheese Sambousek, Spinach Fatayer.
  - **Soup:** Lentil Soup.
  - **Main Course:** 
    - Lamb Ouzi
    - Mixed Grill (Shish Tawook, Lamb Kofta, Lamb Chops)
    - Butter Chicken
    - Fish Sayadiyeh
    - Vegetable Salona
  - **Sides:** Vermicelli Rice, Roasted Potato.
  - **Sweets & Desserts:** Um Ali, Rice Pudding, Baklava Selection, Fresh Fruit Salad, Kunafa, Basbousa.

### Iftar Menu B
- **Price:** 220 AED per person
- **Inclusions:** Includes all items from Menu A with additional premium selections and increased variety in main courses and desserts.

## Sohour Buffet
- **Price:** 125 AED per person
- **Requirements:** Minimum 25 persons.
- **Event Types:** Corporate Event, Private Event.
- **Inclusions:**
  - **Ramadan Beverages:** Jallab, Tamarind, Laban, Lemon with Mint, Arabic Coffee.
  - **Fresh Salad Bar:** Tabbouleh, Fattoush, Hummus, Moutabal, Greek Salad, Corn Salad.
  - **Stations:**
    - **Cheese & Cold Cut Station:** Assorted Cheeses and Halal Cold Cuts.
    - **Live Station:** Foul Medames (with various toppings), Balila, Musabbaha, Fatteh.

  - **Hot Buffet:**
