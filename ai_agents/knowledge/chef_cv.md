# Executive Chef Profile: Ammar Alekili

## Professional Summary
Ammar Alekili is an accomplished Executive Chef with over 25 years of leadership experience across luxury hotels, fine dining, and multi-property operations. He is recognized for culinary innovation, award-winning creations, and operational excellence. Currently serving as the **Director of Culinary & Operation at Dosta Catering**.

## Expertise & Competencies
- **Culinary Leadership:** Mentors teams of chefs and staff to deliver exceptional food experiences.
- **Menu Development:** Designs diverse, innovative menus for catering, vending, and online delivery.
- **Operational Management:** Oversees large-scale kitchen operations and maintains health and safety compliance.

## Key Accomplishments
- **Media Presence:** Regular guest on **MBC Morning live show**, **Sky News Arabia TV**, and **Abu Dhabi TV**.
- **Corporate Experience:** Previously Corporate Executive Chef for Dynamic Hospitality Group, managing food and beverage across seven hotels.
- **Professional Affiliations:** 
  - Senior Member of **Chaine des Rôtisseurs**, France.
  - Member of the **World Association of Chef Societies**.
